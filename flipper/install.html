Install SensorFake

Puts the SensorFake app on your Flipper Zero, straight from this page.

This browser cannot talk to a Flipper

Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux). Phones, Safari and Firefox do not support it.

You can also copy the app by hand: see the README of the project.

Before you start

  1. Connect the Flipper to this computer with a USB cable.
  2. Close qFlipper if it is open.
  3. Leave the Flipper on its main screen.

The browser will ask which device to use. Pick Flipper and press Connect.

  • •Connect to the Flipper
  • •Get the app
  • •Check the SD card
  • •Copy the app
  • •Check the copy
  • •Start SensorFake

Could not download the app automatically. If you have sensorfake.fap on this computer, choose it here and press Install again.

After installing

  1. On the Flipper: Apps → Bluetooth → SensorFake.
  2. In your fitness app, add a heart rate sensor and pick Polar Sense 1234.
  3. OK switches between walk and run. Back quits.
